# Official Scheme Data

This directory contains provenance-first records derived from government/official portals. Every eligibility clause must retain an issuing authority, official URL, last-verified date, document title, and page/section/question reference.

Current records are intentionally conservative:

- PM-USP CSSS: eligibility clauses are sourced from the Ministry of Education's official 2025-26 FAQ; the live National Scholarship Portal 2026-27 status is verified separately. The record is marked for manual review before a final eligibility decision.
- PM-Vidyalaxmi: clauses are sourced from the official PM-Vidyalaxmi portal and Ministry of Education notices; QHEI membership and current conditions must still be checked before a final decision.

Do not place fabricated or unverifiable scheme rules here. Use `scripts/ingest_official.py` to ingest an official PDF/HTML source.
